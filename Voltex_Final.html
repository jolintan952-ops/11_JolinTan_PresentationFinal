Company History

Slide to explore our journey
2010
Founded

Humble Beginnings in a Small Shop

Voltex was founded in a 400 sq ft shopfront in Singapore by two engineers who believed quality electronics shouldn't come at the cost of honest service. Within the first year, we served over 1,200 customers.

2014
Expansion

Launching Our Certified Repair Centre

We opened a dedicated repair facility, staffing manufacturer-certified technicians. The repair division quickly grew to handle over 3,000 devices annually, establishing Voltex as the go-to repair partner for major brands.

2018
Milestone

Awarded Best Electronics Retailer

Voltex received the Consumer Trust Award for Best Electronics Retailer in Southeast Asia, recognising our commitment to product authenticity, fair pricing, and industry-leading customer satisfaction scores.

2021
Digital

Going Online — A New Digital Storefront

We launched our e-commerce platform, allowing customers across Singapore to shop, book repairs, and track their devices online. The digital store saw 50,000 orders in its first year and introduced same-day delivery in the city.

2025
Today

A Premium Brand Built on Trust

Today, Voltex operates multiple locations and serves over 100,000 customers. We continue to expand our product range and repair capabilities, staying true to our founding belief: technology should be honest, reliable, and accessible to all.
